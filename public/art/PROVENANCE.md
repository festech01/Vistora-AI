# Lagoon at dusk

Original procedural illustration of a fictional Nigerian coastal lagoon, with palms, canoes and a cable-stayed bridge. Made for the Vistora AI demonstration. The still, poster and silent five-second camera move share this artwork. No third-party photographs, video, music or likenesses are included. The illustration is released under CC0 1.0 Universal. The sample is illustrative and does not represent output from a paid provider.

The reproducible still generator is in scripts/render-art.py. The movie is an H.264 encoding of a slow push into the still at 768 × 432, 24 fps.

The film reel, layered profile and post-layout card illustrations are original geometric artworks released under the same CC0 dedication. Their source is scripts/render-cards.py.
